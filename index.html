// 02 — Projects

Selected Work

Research and industrial projects in power electronics, control systems, and grid integration.

01
F-UPQC — Flexible Unified Power Quality Conditioner
Grid-tied converter transforming a passive load node into an active grid asset. dq-frame mathematical model, MPC control strategy, EN 50160 / IEC 61000 compliance. PCIM Europe conference paper (IEEEtran, LaTeX).
Active 02
DAB3 — Dual Active Bridge (3-phase)
SPS/EPS/DPS/TPS and Sin-PS modulation strategies for triple-phase DAB. YAB topology with GaN BDS devices. MF transformer loss modeling via Steinmetz/iGSE. Series inductance design and MATLAB frequency-sweep scripts.
Active 03
PMSM Adaptive Observer
Nonlinear adaptive observer for PMSM in the dq-frame. Lyapunov stability proof, state-space discretization, Simulink deployment. FCS-MPC in the alpha-beta frame. Harmonic observer MATLAB class for block mask integration.
Active 04
LeitDrive DPT — Double Pulse Test Campaign
Comparative evaluation of Infineon FF1200R17IP5P vs Danfoss DP650B1700 at iPEL. DriverBoards DB1.00/DB1.51, PSU v1.51/v1.53. Thermal analysis with Mersen pin-fin cold plate. Firmware on Nucleo-G431RB (STM32CubeIDE).
Completed 05
Active Damping — Ropeway Drive Systems
Patented active damping technique for mechanical resonance suppression in high-power ropeway drives. Industrial deployment at Leitner SpA.

// 04 — About

Davide Bagnara

Power Electronics and Control Systems Engineer with ~20 years of experience in industrial R&D. Based in Sterzing (BZ), Italy.

Currently at SECOM srl as Lead Proposal Engineer, sizing custom high-power converters (thyristor rectifiers, IGBT drives, static switches) for industrial and research applications.

Consulting experience at Alintel srl on SiC-based drives and advanced converter topologies. Publications at PCIM Europe and ISIEA; patent holder on active damping for ropeway systems.

Research interests include modern control frameworks (MPC, SMC, PBC, H∞), wide-bandgap semiconductor applications, and the application of machine learning and reinforcement learning to power electronics control.
